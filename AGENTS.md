# AGENTS.md — Gamo Studios

Persistent instructions for anyone (person or agent) working in this repository. Revised 2026-10-19 for the current plan: four products launching together, with Gamo Live and the admin perfected first as the reference for the others, and Gamo Live adding paid ticketing and in-person sales ([`docs/LIVE_REQUIREMENTS.md`](docs/LIVE_REQUIREMENTS.md), [`docs/LIVE_PAYMENTS.md`](docs/LIVE_PAYMENTS.md)). Older rules that described the previous product logic are listed at the end as retired; do not follow them.

**Changing a rule.** A rule here can be changed when it is clearly wrong for the product: change it in this file, record the old rule, the new rule and the reason in [`docs/DECISIONS.md`](docs/DECISIONS.md), and tell the owner. Do not work around a rule silently. The **owner-only actions** in §2 (commits, pushes, deploys, DNS, secrets, real emails, real charges, live Stripe accounts, payouts, destructive remote database operations, paid resources, legal claims) are never changed without the owner.

## 1. Mission and wording (permanent)

**Gamo Studios is a Christian, mission-driven Public Benefit Corporation.** Gamo Worship, Gamo Academy, Gamo Live and Gamo Production are connected programs serving one religious and educational mission, not independent profit centers. The mission reference is [`docs/GAMO_NONPROFIT_MODEL.md`](docs/GAMO_NONPROFIT_MODEL.md) (historical file name; the PBC statement below prevails over any "nonprofit" wording in it).

- A PBC is a for-profit company type. Gamo Studios is **not a nonprofit** and **not tax-exempt**. Never write or imply "nonprofit", "non-profit", "not-for-profit", "501(c)(3)", "tax-exempt", "charitable" or "charity" about Gamo itself, and never say a payment to Gamo is tax-deductible, on any public page, email, metadata or structured data. Never claim IRS approval, registration numbers, a filing status, committed board members, sponsors, donors or partner churches. Say "Public Benefit Corporation" and nothing more about the legal form.
- Judge success by religious and educational impact, accessibility, reliability, musician and instructor fairness, community trust, church collaboration and responsible stewardship. Financial sustainability matters but is subordinate to the mission.
- Without explicit owner approval, never: add or raise fees, turn free or sponsored programs into paid ones, deduct from musician or instructor pay, or let payment, sponsorship or partnership affect vetting, rankings or trust signals. Sponsored content is always visibly labeled.
- Musician pay and any Gamo administrative allocation are always separate line items. All fees, contributions and tiers are proposals until approved.
- **Gifts and sponsorships are allowed with these safeguards** (they replace the old blanket ban on donation and sponsorship processing):
  - a gift always names who receives it ("Add a gift to <organization>"); gifts at Live checkout go to the **organizing church or ministry**, never to Gamo;
  - Gamo makes no tax claim about any payment; if a receiving organization issues tax receipts, that is its own responsibility (the organizer terms say so);
  - every payment is recorded with its money category ([`docs/GAMO_NONPROFIT_MODEL.md`](docs/GAMO_NONPROFIT_MODEL.md) §5.3): money owed to an organization is `third_party_payable`, never Gamo income;
  - Gamo-related payment wording says "gift", not "donation", until counsel says otherwise (an organizer's own description of their gift may say "donation");
  - **gifts to Gamo Studios itself** and paid memberships wait for a legal review of Gamo as a PBC; sponsorship of Academy classes by a church or any organization is part of the product.
- Gamo Live serves **Christian events nationwide**; never define eligibility by ethnicity or nationality.
- Gamo Worship books musicians for **any occasion** (a service, a concert, an event or anything else, on any day) and for **almost any person or group**, not only churches.
- Gamo Academy is **for anyone who wants to learn an instrument, plus production training** (audio, video and technical setup); classes and workshops can be sponsored by churches or any organization, and fully sponsored classes can be free to the learner. The approved headline is **"Learn to Play the Instrument You Love."** Gamo Production may mention training but sends people into Academy for it.
- **Every partner is verified through a meeting** with Gamo (a video call or in person): churches, other groups, musicians and providers, and Gamo Live organizations. "Verified" appears only after the admin recorded the meeting and the decision.
- No corporate growth language ("monetization engine", "revenue optimization", "acquisition funnel", "customer lifetime value").
- Email: the verified sending domain is `gamostudio.org` (contact `hello@gamostudio.org`); `scripts/audit/email-domain.test.ts` fails on the misspelled extra-"s" domain.

## 2. How the owner works with you (permanent)

- **Tests: targeted yes, broad only when asked.** Write and update tests alongside the code, and run the specific test files (or single Playwright specs) that cover what you just changed, once per change set, to know the work is right. Do not run the full unit suite, `pnpm check`, `check:quick`, the full Playwright suite or the journeys on your own initiative (not while iterating, not before a commit, push or deploy); run those only when the owner asks, or at the end of a planned phase the owner has approved. Never loop on re-running. State plainly in reports which tests were run and which were not. Builds, typechecks, lint and format checks are always fine.
- **Commits:** never run `git commit`. When work is ready, give a ready-to-paste command (`git add …` and `git commit -m "…"`) with a short one-line message, no body, no attribution trailers.
- **Never** deploy, push to a remote, change DNS, change secrets or security settings, spend money, send real emails, charge cards, run payouts, run destructive database operations, or publish legal terms as final without the owner's explicit approval for that action.
- **Dev servers belong to the owner.** Never stop, kill or restart a dev server. Test against the owner's running server (read-only), or ask them to start one. Astro allows one dev server per app; after dependency changes, tell the owner to restart theirs.
- Never add a provider or external service (analytics, AI APIs, messaging, payment, auth vendors…) without approval. **Approved:** Stripe, including Stripe Connect and Stripe Terminal, in **test mode** (2026-10-19); live mode needs the owner. Propose anything else (for example a wallet-pass signer) to the owner instead of adding it.
- Never present invented screenshots or test results. Browser evidence comes from a running UI.
- **No fake data or counts, ever.** Every number, badge and list comes from a real query; empty states say so honestly. Nothing appears in navigation without a working page behind it. Fictional people, bookings, events or stories are visibly labeled *Sample*/*Example*.
- Record meaningful progress in [`docs/BUILD_LOG.md`](docs/BUILD_LOG.md) and decisions in [`docs/DECISIONS.md`](docs/DECISIONS.md) (both are history logs; this file holds the rules).
- **Use parallel agents when work splits cleanly.** When a task has independent parts that touch different files (for example shared components, a security pass and a data-accuracy pass), run them as parallel agents with explicit file ownership and a written brief, then review and integrate the results yourself. Do not split work that edits the same files or depends on an earlier step. Agents follow this file too (no running tests, no commits, no touching dev servers).
- **Visual design is owner-led.** The owner decides how screens look, one screen at a time, from screenshots. Describe a visual change and wait for a go when it is big or ambiguous; make no unasked visual changes. The marketing pages (including Live's home, events, event detail, artists, venues, header, footer and event card) stay exactly as they are unless the owner names the page and the change; check with `git diff 98719de --stat` over the marketing paths before reporting done. Parallel agents are for work that splits cleanly by file (logic, tests, scripts, docs), not for restyling the same screens.
- Plan briefly, then work through the agreed scope. Stop for the owner on visual sign-off, owner-only actions and real decisions; otherwise make ordinary engineering choices yourself.

## 3. Scope of the rebuild

- **Four equal products launch together** (Worship, Academy, Live, Production) with the admin that runs them. **Gamo Live and the admin are perfected first** and become the reference: their components, conventions and feature patterns (payment interface, ledger, fee display, door-staff links, reports, meeting-based verification) are built so the other three apps can reuse them, in `packages/*` rather than inside `apps/live`.
- **Gamo Live now includes paid ticketing (online and at the door)** alongside free events, free registration and the editorial catalog of external listings ([`docs/LIVE_REQUIREMENTS.md`](docs/LIVE_REQUIREMENTS.md)).
- **Kept as they are (do not touch):** the marketing pages of the five public apps (look and content). For Gamo Live that means every public page and its chrome: home, events list, event detail, artists, venues, the header, footer and event card, plus their copy (the Promote page was removed; `/promote` redirects to `/organizer`). The one approved exception is the state-aware action panel on the event detail page, which gains states for Gamo's own tickets (smallest possible change, using the panel's own markup). A polish or rebuild pass never changes them unless the owner names a specific page and change; if a task seems to need it, ask first. Before finishing any task, compare the marketing files with `git diff` against the starting commit and confirm they are unchanged. The only parts of the old Live public side that are rebuilt are the non-marketing flows (registration, confirmation, ticket page, sign-in, organizer, admin), and the admin's Cloudflare Access gate and shell (`apps/admin`, [`docs/ADMIN_REQUIREMENTS.md`](docs/ADMIN_REQUIREMENTS.md)).
- **Being rebuilt from scratch:** every non-marketing page (accounts, onboarding, dashboards, flows), every API route, the shared backend (`@gamo/domain`, product sign-in in `@gamo/auth`, the data model in `@gamo/db`, `@gamo/integrations`, the API contracts), demo-era UI in `@studio-gamo/ui`, and their tests. Until a product is rebuilt, its old code may still exist; it is not a reference for how things should work.
- **Order:** Gamo Live and the admin first, then the other three products, all launching together. Each product goes requirements → design → build → built-worker checks, like the admin.
- **Production is frozen** for the public apps until each rebuilt product is ready and the owner approves a deploy.

## 4. Engineering rules (permanent)

- **Stack:** Astro (server output) on Cloudflare Workers, D1, R2, KV; Tailwind 4; strict TypeScript; pnpm workspaces. Check installed versions and vendor docs before using framework commands.
- **Hosts and ports** come from `packages/config/apps.ts`; cross-app links from `packages/ui/src/lib/sites.ts`. Never hard-code an origin.
- **Runtime config:** read bindings and secrets through `getRuntimeEnv()`, never `import.meta.env`. New variables go in `packages/config/env.ts`, `env-validation.ts` (when required), the app's `.dev.vars.example` and the runbook.
- **Fail closed.** A missing key, binding or identity means the feature is unavailable (503/denied), never a fake success. Local-only flags (`ALLOW_DEMO_SESSION`, `ALLOW_AUTH_SIMULATION`, `ALLOW_PUBLIC_DEMO_WORKSPACES`, `ALLOW_LOCAL_ADMIN_IDENTITY`, `GAMO_DEV_PHONE_TOKEN`) are fatal outside `ENVIRONMENT=local`.
- **Every state-changing request** checks identity first, then same origin, then validates input. The frontend is never the authority for permissions, inventory, check-in or payments.
- **CSP:** no inline `<script>` or `on*=` handlers; scripts and styles ship as files; fonts self-hosted; no third-party script origin without approval. Security headers come from `packages/domain/src/http/security-headers.ts` (moves with the rebuild; keep it at least as strict).
- **Money** in integer minor units with the currency recorded. **Time** zones handled explicitly.
- **Data:** schema changes are tracked migrations; never edit an applied migration; nothing destructive without the owner.
- **Logs** name variables and error codes, never secret values or personal data. Screenshots and fixtures never show secrets, ID documents or real personal data.
- **Minimal client JavaScript:** server-rendered pages and plain forms; islands only for real interaction.
- **Thin pages:** no application or API logic in `.astro` page files (database writes, email sending, business rules, multi-step flows). Rules and SQL live in `@gamo/domain`; orchestration such as "cancel and notify" lives in the app's `src/lib/`. A page parses the request, calls one named function, and renders or redirects with the result. Every such function has a unit test.
- **Components:** compose pages from small typed components — shared primitives in `packages/ui`, app-specific components in the app's `src/components/`, large page sections extracted into their own components. No monolithic page files. **Reuse first:** before writing UI or a feature, search `packages/ui` and the app's `src/components/` for something that already does it; extend it instead of copying. The second time a pattern (button, notice, pill, empty state, form row, card, list row, page header) appears, it becomes a shared component, and the earlier copies are replaced in the same change. No page repeats markup or logic another page already has.
- **Dev servers are reachable from a phone.** Every `make dev-<app>` target must print both the this-computer URL and the same-Wi-Fi network URL and serve on both (with `GAMO_DEV_LAN=1`, honored by `createGamoDevServer`), all through the shared helper `scripts/dev/print-urls.sh`, so a new app's dev target gets this by using the helper. The admin is the one exception, on purpose: its local identity stand-in is for this computer only, so `make dev-admin` prints this-computer only and never listens on the network. To see the admin on a phone, `make admin-phone` serves it behind a random secret link that is copied to the clipboard.
- **Removed or renamed public URLs** keep a redirect (`src/lib/legacy-redirects.ts` in each app).

## 5. Design (permanent)

- One design system, defined in [`DESIGN.md`](DESIGN.md) and the tokens in `packages/ui/src/styles/tokens.css` (terracotta `#B34E24`, deep `#8F3B18`, soft `#D98B62`, ink `#18181B`, cream `#F9F6F0`, sand `#EDE6DA`, tan `#D8C5AE`); Inter, self-hosted.
- Every page: exactly one `<h1>`, interactive targets at least 44 × 44 px, axe WCAG 2.2 AA clean (serious/critical), no horizontal overflow at 360 px.
- Marketing pages follow the cinematic editorial design and the approved references in `design-reference/` (homepage sections `design-reference/homepage/02-…07-`, approved hero preserved). Rebuild screens get their own approved design before they are built.
- **Dashboard pages are assembled from separate floating cards.** In the organizer area and the admin, a page file only lists components: a title, a toolbar card (search and filters), summary cards, one card per list row or per topic, the calendar card. No card contains another card's contents (a search box, its filters and the list are three things, not one panel), and nothing is hard-coded in a page that another page could reuse. Each card is a component (app-specific in `src/components/`, shared in `packages/ui`: `Panel`, `Stat`, `Calendar`, `SearchField`, `FilterChips`, `EmptyState`, `Button`, `Pill`), so a new screen is composed, not drawn. Cards float on the page background with space between them; do not overdo it with extra decoration.
- **Dashboards behave like dashboards.** Menus and popovers close on a press outside and on Escape (focus returns to their button), only one is open at a time, pressable things show a pointer, hover, focus and pressed state, forms cannot be submitted twice by a double click, and everything works from the keyboard. This comes from the shared `packages/ui/src/scripts/interactions.ts` and `styles/interactions.css`, loaded by every dashboard layout; a new dashboard layout loads them too. Confirm panels are never dismissed by a press outside.
- **Every number is a link to what it counts.** A count on a dashboard opens the list it counts, with the same filter applied, and the number equals the length of that list (`apps/live/src/lib/organizer-stats.ts` holds the mapping for Live). A chip with a count uses the same query as its list. A count with nothing behind it is not shown.
- **The shell is the same everywhere.** A dashboard has a collapsible sidebar (shared `packages/ui/src/scripts/sidebar-collapse.ts`, one storage key per app) that collapses to an icon rail with labels kept for screen readers and shown as tooltips; the signed-in person's menu sits at the bottom of the sidebar (name, email, account or sign-out) and as one avatar button in the phone top bar; the collapsed logo is an inline SVG in the brand orange (the favicon turns cream in a dark browser theme); phones get a bottom tab bar. The top bar carries no name, pill or duplicate of the sidebar.
- **Accessible names come from visible text.** Never an `aria-label` that differs from the visible words (axe `label-content-name-mismatch`): add the extra words as `sr-only` text inside the control (a count badge reads "3 waiting", a stat card reads "Opens …"). Do not fade text with `opacity`; use solid colours that pass contrast. State is never colour alone.
- **Dates are a calendar, and view state lives in the URL.** Anything date-heavy (an event's dates, an organization's schedule) uses the shared model-driven `Calendar` from `packages/ui` with `packages/domain/src/calendar.ts` (`view`, `pick`, `month`, `week`, `date`, `year` query parameters, `#anchor` links), server-rendered, no client script. Do not build a second calendar or a long list of dates.
- **Dangerous actions use `ConfirmAction`** (a compact danger button that opens an honest consequence with real numbers, then a real POST), placed with the page's other actions; a press outside never dismisses it. Pages that carry a secret in the URL (invitation, ticket, confirmation links) send `Referrer-Policy: same-origin`, not `no-referrer` (browsers then send `Origin: null` and same-origin checks reject the form).
- **Check sizes in a browser, against the Live scale, before calling a dashboard screen done.** Type and element sizes are part of the screen, not an afterthought: page title 22 to 26 px, page description 14 px, card titles 15 to 16 px, row titles 15 px, labels and meta 12 to 13 px, big numbers about 28 px, stat cards and buttons compact, sidebar about 250 px (88 px collapsed), content at most about 1100 px wide. Look at the running page at 1440 and 1280 px wide and a 700 px tall laptop, with the sidebar open and collapsed, and with every menu and popover open: nothing is cut off, clipped or hidden under another element. **Never put a popover or menu inside an element with `overflow` set (scrolling or hidden)**; put the scroll on the inner list and keep the container visible, with a `z-30` stacking level so content does not paint over the menu.
- **Organize by task and relationship, not by page count.** Not every screen gets a sidebar item. The sidebar holds a handful of areas (about four to six); screens that serve one job or are used together are grouped under one area and reached through tabs (shared `FilterChips` with real counts) inside it, and rarely used screens live in the area they belong to rather than in the menu. Before adding a screen, decide which area it belongs to and what the person was just doing: a decision list links to the thing and returns to the list; a count opens its list; the next step is on the screen where the last one ends (check in from the day's schedule, review from the calendar, back to the queue after a decision). Detail pages keep their area highlighted and offer a way back. The desktop sidebar and the phone tab bar show the same areas. Describe the structure (areas, tabs, the main tasks and how many presses each takes) to the owner before building a new dashboard or adding an area.
- **Split-screen pages (sign-in, sign-up, registration, confirmation, organizer set-up, invitations) never scroll on desktop.** The page is exactly one screen tall, content centered, photo on the left. If the fields do not fit, reduce type and spacing a little, then split the form into steps instead of shrinking further: a thin progress bar with "Step N of M", a Next button, a Back link, each step a server-rendered page that validates its own fields and keeps earlier answers. Phones may scroll. Check at laptop heights (about 700 and 900 px) before calling any such page done.
- **Admin:** claymorphism shapes and shadows from `design-reference/claymorphism-admin-ui/` in the same palette, full-page layout, collapsible sidebar, Gamo Studios logo with an "Admin" label, **no gradients anywhere**.

## 6. Admin access (permanent)

One admin, one account. Cloudflare Access alone: every admin request must carry a valid Access assertion for exactly `ADMIN_EMAIL`; anything else is denied. No admin accounts, roles, passwords, sessions or emergency credentials. Recovery is changing the Access policy in Cloudflare. Locally, `ALLOW_LOCAL_ADMIN_IDENTITY=true` on a loopback host stands in for Access.

## 7. Payments and money (permanent)

Details in [`docs/LIVE_PAYMENTS.md`](docs/LIVE_PAYMENTS.md); security baselines in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

- **Stripe test mode only** until the owner approves production. Without keys, paid sales fail closed; the local Stripe stand-in runs only with `ENVIRONMENT=local` (and in the test harness). Live keys (`sk_live_…`) are refused outside production, test keys inside it.
- **Two funds-flow modes behind one interface.** Mode A: the organization's own Stripe account (Connect direct charges; the organization is the seller of record). Mode B: Gamo collects on its own account and pays the organization out (Connect transfers). The mode is a platform default with per-organization overrides, set in the admin; until the owner chooses, it is "not chosen" and paid sales are unavailable. Pages and orchestration never branch on the mode; they call the payment interface. Stripe is never described as escrow.
- **Fees and gifts are their own line items.** The Gamo fee is computed, stored and shown separately, starts at $0, is an admin setting whose every change is a recorded decision, and is never charged on free tickets, cash sales or gifts unless the owner decides otherwise. The organizer chooses per event whether the buyer pays it ("pass on") or the organizer absorbs it.
- **Snapshots at purchase.** The price, the fee, the policies (refund, transfer, re-entry) and the seller of record are copied onto the order when it is created; a later change applies to existing orders only when it is better for the buyer.
- **The verified webhook is the authority** for payment; a browser returning from Stripe never confirms anything. Inventory is reserved by the database before payment.
- **A ledger** records every money movement as typed entries in integer cents with currency; reports and statements read from it, never from ad-hoc sums.
- **No card surcharges** added for buyers. **Transfers yes, resale no:** a transfer has no price field and there is no marketplace.
- **Payouts and live connected accounts** are owner-only actions; the admin prepares them, the owner approves.

## 8. Tools and helper agents

- `.agents/agents/` holds the owner's Antigravity helper agents (`design-lead`, `frontend-builder`, `ui-reviewer`); they follow this file. Antigravity agents prefer the workspace's native file tools over external filesystem tools and never bypass workspace permissions.
- In an agent shell, run Astro commands with `env -u CLAUDECODE -u AI_AGENT -u CLAUDE_CODE_ENTRYPOINT` so they stay in the foreground.
