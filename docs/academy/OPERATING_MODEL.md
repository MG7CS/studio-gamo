# Gamo Academy — recommended operating model

Status: **proposal for owner decision** (2026-11-16). Built from the seven files in [`research/`](research/). Ideal-first: this describes the most recommended setup; constraints come after. Nothing here sets a fee, tier or contribution; those stay proposals until the owner approves them. Teacher pay and any Gamo administrative allocation are always separate lines.

## 1. The model in one page

- **One weekly visit per student:** a 45-minute class, then a 60-minute supervised practice hour, same site, same cohort. The family member who brought the student stays or waits in the parent corner.
- **Small same-level groups:** 4–6 students, never fewer than 3, one instrument per student (keyboard with headphones; practice pads for drums first).
- **Terms:** 12 weeks, three intakes a year, 36 teaching weeks, a one-year first commitment, a showcase and progress review every term.
- **Three phases:** A fully supported (class plus on-site practice), B blended (less on-site time, more home practice), C independent (own instrument at home, monthly masterclass and a termly ensemble day). Graduating students free seats for new students.
- **Ages:** no instrument lessons at 4–5 (an optional parent-and-child Foundations class instead); bands 6–8, 9–12, 13–17 and adults, each with its own cohort.
- **Adults** get the same structure (60-minute class plus 60-minute lab), three tracks (beginner, returner, church fast track), and a playable song in session one.
- **Sites** are partner-hosted (church, school or organization) under three documents (MOU, facility-use agreement, safeguarding addendum); a site goes live only after a safeguarding gate.
- **Measure from day one** (attendance, retention at 3, 6 and 12 months, minutes of individual attention); do not publish outcome claims until Gamo has its own data.

## 3. Weekly shape by age

| Band | Class | Supervised hour (active playing) | Home practice | Parent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4–5 (optional Foundations) | 30 min, parent and child | n/a | listening, singing | in the room |
| 6–8 | 45 min, groups of 4–6 | 20–25 min of the hour | 10–15 min, 5 days | in the room |
| 9–12 | 45–60 min, groups of 4–8 | 30–35 min | 20–30 min, 5 days | on site, last 5–10 min |
| 13–17 | 60 min ending in an ensemble | 40–45 min | 25–30 min+, 5 days | drop-off allowed by policy |
| Adults | 60 min, 4–6 | 45–50 min | 20 min, 5 days | n/a |

Target practice standard across ages: **5 days a week, one rest day**; 4 days is the minimum for steady progress; short frequent sessions beat long rare ones (a guidance, not a controlled-study result).

## 4. The supervised practice hour

- **Shape:** goal card, warm-up, two or three practice blocks, a break, a peer round, practice log, 3-minute handoff to the parent.
- **Mentors:** 1 per 6 students by default; 1 per 4 for beginner voice and string-type instruments; 1 per 8 at headphone stations; a check-in round every 12 minutes; 2-hour training and a 10-minute weekly briefing; two screened adults always present; under-18 helpers never count toward them.
- **Space:** about 25 square feet per headphone station, 35–40 per acoustic player (300–450 for 12 students, 600–900 for 24) plus one to three small rooms (65–120 sq ft) for voice and loud instruments; never simultaneous acoustic practice in a reverberant sanctuary.
- **Hearing:** room at or below 80 dBA; pads or electronic drums first; earmuffs and a sound-level meter on site.
- **Tracking without accounts:** paper goal-and-log card, a short staff form, the admin dashboard for attendance and flags, a three-line weekly email to the family. No leaderboards.

## 5. Pathway, hours and the move to home practice

| Instrument | Phase A (weeks, fully supported) | Phase B (weeks, blended) | Take home |
|---|---|---|---|
| Piano | 16 (range 12–24) | 20 | digital piano after the Home-Ready Check |
| Guitar / bass | 12 (8–16) | 24 | guitar after the check |
| Drums | 16 | 20 | practice pad from week 1; kit stays on site |
| Voice | 8 | 28 | nothing to take home; practice from week 1 |

- **Phase C:** monthly 60-minute masterclass, one ensemble day a term, home practice about 120 minutes a week.
- **Hours:** about 90–105 learning hours by week 36 (Levels 0–1); Levels 0–4 total about 450 hours over about 148 weeks (about 3 years). Published reference: Trinity Grade 1 is 60 total hours (12 teacher-led), about 80% independent practice.
- **Home-Ready Check:** five parts (skills, attendance, practice-log consistency, family commitment, instrument fit); release on the check, never on time alone.
- **Home instruments:** weighted digital pianos about $400–$500; a take-home loan pool depreciates about $100–$140 a year per loaner versus $216–$420 a year to rent from a retailer; written loan agreement with care and damage terms; no loan tied to fees without owner approval.
- **Capacity example:** 8 stations, 20 open hours a week, 80% use is 128 station-hours a week; with graduation that supports about 54 new starts a year (three intakes of 18) and a roster of about 160 by year three, versus about 73 if no one ever graduates. Reserve about 10% of seats for step-back returns; waitlist by application date only.

## 6. Adults and church pathways

- Three tracks: **Beginner** (12 weeks), **Returner** (12 weeks after a 20-minute placement chat), **Church Fast Track** (8 weeks plus 4 rehearsals); optional 6-week refresher; weekday 6:30–8:30 pm and Saturday mornings.
- About 80% playing, 20% just-in-time theory; first four weeks on three-chord worship songs and hymns in two keys; one student-chosen song a month.
- Worship pathway about 50–70 supervised hours (35–40 on the fast track); tech pathway about 40 hours over at least 3 months. **Gamo issues a skills-ready letter; the church decides who serves.** No ranking among adult students.
- Monthly one-hour Family Jam with tiered parts; grandparent workshops as an experiment.

## 7. Sites, partners and safety

- **Host fit:** enough rooms, sound isolation, outlets, instrument storage, restrooms, safe drop-off and pickup, ADA access, a parent waiting area; score with the site-readiness rubric in research file 06.
- **Documents:** MOU, facility-use agreement, safeguarding addendum. Gamo handles registration, payments, teachers and curriculum; the host handles the building; no cash changes hands at the host; host payment or sponsorship never affects vetting or teacher selection.
- **Safeguarding is a gate:** signed agreements, certificates of insurance (host named as additional insured), completed screening and training, and an emergency drill before any site opens. Follow the CDC youth-serving organization components; at least two screened adults at all times; authorized-adult list and ID check at pickup; incident reports; photo and video consent.
- **Insurance** (broker and counsel items): abuse-and-molestation coverage is hard to obtain in 2026 (published premium examples about $815–$8,981 a year).
- **Licensing:** a CCLI license covers copying music for singing, not performing it; get counsel before any recital, recording or stream.
- **Standard site kit:** roughly $10,000–$15,000 (unverified); about $10.4k, $19.5k and $38.6k to equip a 24-, 48- or 96-student site per research file 07.
- **Multi-city playbook:** a 12-week launch calendar, a city-selection rubric, and per-site metrics (enrollment, attendance, retention, waitlist, satisfaction).
- **Later (deferred):** a student bus: funding sources, federal and state classification, driver licensing, insurance and the FMCSA financial-responsibility thresholds, all for counsel.

## 8. Unit economics (proposals for the financial model)

From research file 07: one 45-minute class of 6 with one lead plus one 60-minute practice session, 36 weeks (63 hours a year per student).

| | 24 students | 48 students | 96 students |
|---|---|---|---|
| Direct cost per student per year | $932 | $831 | $750 |
| Capital per site (fleet plus loaners) | about $10.4k | about $19.5k | about $38.6k |
| Break-even tuition per month (60/25/15 payer mix) | $148 | $132 | $119 |
| Support target per student at $75/month standard | $460 | $359 | $277 |

Benchmarks: El Sistema USA averaged $2,401.68 per student in 2023–24 (71% of programs charge no tuition; the median fee among the rest is $263); Harmony Project $1,500 (2015); community-school private lessons $41–$101 per lesson; group classes $22–$53 per class; teacher pay about $28–$32 an hour. Free host space is worth about $281–$375 per student per year; cash rent of about $25 per room-hour adds that much. Insurance, background checks, payroll burden, tax and certificate costs are unverified placeholders. **Funding for a PBC (grants, investments, sponsorships) needs counsel and accountant review.**

## 10. Decisions needed from the owner

1. Approve **one weekly visit** (class plus supervised hour) as the standard, with optional mid-week open practice.
2. Approve **12-week terms, three intakes, a one-year first commitment**.
3. Approve **45 minutes** as the standard class length (30, 45 or 60 by band).
4. Approve **groups of 4–6** and the **mentor ratio 1:6** as the default, and mentor pay and structure (volunteer, stipend or paid).
5. Approve the **Foundations (ages 4–5)** optional class, or defer it.
6. Which instruments launch first (recommendation: piano, then guitar; drums on pads; voice).
7. Take-home instrument policy: **loan pool**, rent-to-own or family purchase; who owns the loan agreement.
8. Host types for the pilot (church, school, organization) and the **first city**.
9. Safeguarding standard (two screened adults, screening vendor), and who holds insurance (host, Gamo or both).
10. Whether to set **tuition** at all, a sliding scale and a support target (proposals only until approved).
11. Pilot size (12 or 24 students) and how long before opening a second site.
12. Recital and recording rules and the licensing path.

## 11. What this means for the product and the financial model

- **Admin records (staff-managed, no public accounts):** Site (host, rooms, schedule, readiness score, agreement dates, insurance certificates), Cohort (instrument, level, capacity, teacher, term), Inventory ledger (instruments, loans, returns). These belong in the admin app alongside the forms, roster and invoice work already planned.
- **Forms (shared `DynamicForm`):** host inquiry and site readiness; registration and waitlist (allergies, authorized adults, photo consent); authorized-adult change; incident report (staff-only); instrument loan and return; end-of-term survey; Home-Ready Check.
- **Emails:** enrollment confirmation, waitlist offer, schedule and weather changes, weekly three-line practice note, recital information, safeguarding follow-ups; subjects never carry names.
- **Financial model inputs (to be loaded as assumptions, not conclusions):** class of 6, mentor 1:6, 63 hours per student per year, instrument depreciation $100–$140 per loaner per year, equipment prices from file 07, the three site sizes, free or rented hosting, and an administrative allocation shown as its own variable line.
- **Evidence honesty:** most sizes and ratios are judgment; record Gamo's own data from the first session and replace judgment with it by the end of year one.
