## Summary

1. **The closest published cost benchmark is El Sistema USA.** Its 2023-24 member census (84 of 142 programs answered, US and Canada) reports an average of **$2,401.68 per student per year** at about **7 hours of instruction per week**; programs with 0-50 students averaged **$4,712** and programs with 51-100 averaged **$3,404** per student [S1]. In 2021-22 the figure was $3,501 per student and **$22.49 per student-hour** [S2]. Gamo's lighter format (1.75 hours a week) models at **$750-$930 per student per year** before the Gamo administrative allocation, which is about $12-$15 per student-hour. That is below the $22.49 benchmark, so the model is plausible but not padded.
2. **71% of El Sistema USA programs charge no tuition**; the 29% that do charge a median of **$263 a year** (mean $890, range $20-$6,000) [S1]. The membership standard is "tuition free or scholarship based" with ensemble work at the core [S3]. A church-hosted Gamo that charges **$75 a month** (9 months = $675 a year) would sit well above that median but **below** typical group-class prices in community music schools ($23-$53 per class, see section 2).
3. **Private lessons in 2026-27 community schools run about $41-$77 per lesson in Rochester NY and $50-$101 in Minneapolis** (30 to 60 minutes) [S14, S15]. A rural Virginia church academy charges **$25 per 30 minutes** [S23]. Gamo's group class (6 students) plus supervised practice is priced against group classes, not private lessons.
4. **Instructor pay evidence:** US music-teacher averages are **$28 an hour (Salary.com, Nov 2026, middle half $24-$31)** [S27] and **$31.68 an hour (Indeed, Nov 2026, range $16.90-$59.38)** [S26]. The model pays lead teachers **$45 per paid hour** (a deliberate proposal above the averages, covering prep and transitions), mentors **$22** and the site coordinator **$25**. No source gave assistant or mentor rates; those are judgment [K].
5. **Free hosting is the biggest single cost lever.** A church standard classroom rents for about **$25 an hour** (Leawood KS, effective Aug 1 2026) [S29]; other church schedules show **$10-$20 an hour** [S30\*]. Gamo's three sites use **9, 20 and 30 room-hours a week**, which would be **$8,100, $18,000 and $27,000 a year at $25** if hosting were cash. In-kind hosting is therefore worth **$281-$375 per student per year**, more than a third of the instruction cost. Record it as in-kind value, not cash.
6. **Equipment (US, new, Nov 16 2026):** Yamaha P-45 88-key weighted piano **$399.99** [S31]; Sweetwater's bundle with stand, bench and pedal **$559** [S47\*]; Yamaha PSR-E383 61-key **$169.99** [S32]; Yamaha C40 classical guitar **$179.99** [S33]; FG800J steel-string **$279.99** [S34]; Fender Frontman 10G practice amp **$89.99** [S37]; Evans 12-inch practice pad **$24.99** [S40]; Alesis Nitro Max electronic kit **$399** [S42]; 4-channel headphone amp **$24.90** [S38]. Take-home rental market: **$18-$35 a month** (community lender tiers [S51], [S50]) up to $55 (a Canadian retailer, electric guitar [S\*]).
7. **Three-site model (proposal, admin allocation shown separately):** direct cost **$22,373 (24 students, $932 each), $39,897 (48 students, $831) and $71,973 (96 students, $750)**. At a $75 monthly standard tuition with a 60/25/15 full/half/free payer mix, tuition covers **51%, 57% and 63%** of cost; the remaining **$277-$460 per student** needs support from churches, companies or investment, not from musician pay. Break-even tuition with that mix is **$148, $132 and $119 a month**; if every family paid full price it would be **$107, $96 and $86**.
8. **For-profit status limits grants.** Most foundations restrict grants to tax-exempt entities. Foundations can reach for-profits through program-related investments, mission-related investments, or grants under "expenditure responsibility", and a January 2025 Stanford guide estimates **under 5% of foundation funds** go to for-profits [S54]. Whether Gamo as a PBC qualifies for any given source, and how gifted instruments or sponsorships are described, **needs counsel and accountant review** before any outreach.

### 1.2 What the programs teach Gamo (copy / avoid)

**El Sistema USA and Sistema-inspired programs**
- Format: intensive ensemble, long hours, mostly free [S1][S3]. Copy: the idea that the cost driver is staff time, and that **small programs cost more per student** (the 0-50 bin is $4,712 versus $2,249 for 701+ in 2023-24 [S1]). That supports sharing the $600 a year platform and training across sites. Copy: alumni as paid mentors (67% employ alumni [S1]); the Academy's mentor role can become a paid pathway for older students. Avoid: copying the 5-7 hour weekly load; the Gamo format is 1.75 hours a week, which suits families and church schedules.
- Gap: the census text I extracted does not give student-teacher ratios or retention. Gamo should record and publish its own attendance and retention from year one.

**Harmony Project**
- Copy: an instrument to take home, ensemble performance, wraparound support (counselors, tutoring, per a California Arts Council listing [S5\*]), and free to families. The 2014 Kraus study followed children for two years and found that **attendance and participation** (engagement) predicted stronger neural speech encoding [S6\*]. It had no active control group and no stated retention figure. Avoid: quoting the "97% to college" claim; it is an organization claim from a 2015 TV piece, not an evaluation [S4].
- Cost anchor: $1,500 per student (2015); inflation alone pushes that well above $2,000 today (judgment [K]).

**Little Kids Rock / Music Will**
- Copy: chord-first teaching that gets children playing songs quickly; free instruments; training teachers rather than only hiring them; a band rehearsal as the reward [S7]. Copy: a school-launch cost of tens of dollars per student when it trains existing teachers (only for the in-school model, not directly comparable to Gamo).
- Avoid: comparing that low number with Gamo. It works because the school pays the teacher; Gamo pays its own teachers and pays rent in kind.

**Guitars Over Guns**
- Copy: paid professional musicians as mentors, small ensembles, two sessions a week. Avoid: using their outcomes claims; all are self-reported [S10]. Cost is not published, so there is no cost benchmark.

**Music for Life (identification uncertain)**
- Several programs use this name. The one the search returned runs free after-school lessons for eligible youth in the Washington DC area [S\* search result]. I could not find hours, ratios or cost, so I give no benchmark. Do not cite it.

**People's Music School**
- Copy: a **family volunteer commitment of at least 4 hours a semester** [S12], which lowers coordinator load and builds ownership; 15-20 hours a year of paid faculty development; annual assessments of musical and social-emotional progress. Avoid: assuming a no-tuition model is financially stable without a large, steady support base; it is 100% philanthropic [S12].

**Community schools of the arts (National Guild)**
- Copy: **formal, published financial aid policy** (Guild-profiled members include Community Music Center SF, Flint School of Performing Arts, Rhode Island Philharmonic Music School and Sherwood Community Music School [S13]). Eastman publishes a need-and-merit aid process with fixed deadlines (Aug 1 and Dec 1) and partner programs for local districts [S15]. Avoid: tax-return-based proof of need for church families who may not file in the usual way; use a simple self-declaration plus a church or school referral (judgment [K]).

**Yamaha Music School and Kindermusik (franchise or licensed models)**
- Copy: a **single weekly group class on a fixed term** with a materials kit; clear per-term price. Avoid: franchise royalty structures. For scale, the School of Rock franchise cost is reported as $387,050-$756,100 plus an 8% royalty [S17]; extracting revenue shares is contrary to the Gamo model.

**Church music academies and worship-arts programs**
- Copy: Brentwood's rules (background checks, instructors active in the church's worship ministry), gifted instruments refurbished for students, a small scholarship fund [S22]. Copy: Hosanna's "path to the worship team" for older students [S24\*]. Avoid: relying on 1:1 lessons only; at $25-$34 per half hour they cannot serve 24-96 students affordably (Gamo's group format costs about $14-$26 per student per week, section 4).

**School-district band and guitar classes**
- The one district study I could open found $187 per student, with salaries as the main cost (the 10-district NAMM-funded study reported by search summary said 85.4% of music spending went to educator salaries and $251 per student [S55\*]). Copy: the lesson that the teacher is 80%+ of the cost; **do not underfund teachers**.

**Retention evidence (general)**
- A 2024 study (Tucker) cited in a 2026 Frontiers paper found **over half of students left school music classes after one year** (band, orchestra, guitar) [S56\*]. I found no source that compares group and private retention. Gamo should set a retention target and measure it. (A commonly repeated "90% of new guitar students quit in the first year" figure traces to blogs, not a study; do not use it.)

### 2.3 Term lengths, siblings, scholarships, sliding scale

- **Term lengths seen:** 14 weeks (Brentwood), 15 (Kindermusik), 16 (Eastman, Hyland Heights), 17 (MacPhail), 18 classes (Crowden), 34 weeks (Music Settlement, Cleveland, 2026-27) [S14][S15][S16][S20][S21][S22][S23]. Gamo's 36-week year (three 12-week terms) is within the common 32-36 week school year.
- **Sibling discounts:** Cleveland Music Settlement offers sibling, senior and multiple-registration discounts (one per family per fiscal year) [S21]; one Kindermusik studio charges $70 per month for the first child and $55 for additional children [S\* search result]. Brentwood offers **no** multi-child discount but offers partial scholarships [S22]; MacPhail and Eastman pages list none [S14][S15]. Proposal for Gamo: a simple family cap, for example the third and later siblings at no charge, set by the owner.
- **Scholarship and sliding-scale models:**
  - Eastman: need plus merit, using page 1 of a federal tax return, fixed deadlines, district partnerships (about 70 students in Eastman-Pathways) [S15].
  - Brentwood: small number of first-come scholarships each semester [S22].
  - Cleveland Music Settlement: hardship assistance and merit auditions [S21].
  - People's Music School: free tuition, but families give at least 4 volunteer hours a semester [S12].
  - Gift of Music (Atlanta, instruments, not lessons): three tiers at $35, $18 and $0 a month, based on benefit enrollment or income verification [S51].
  - El Sistema USA: 71% charge nothing; among those that charge, the median is $263 a year [S1].

## 4. Per-student annual cost model (three scenarios)

All numbers below are **proposals and assumptions**, not approved fees or pay. The model is meant to be pasted into the owner's planning tool; formulas use plain names so each can become a spreadsheet cell.

### 4.1 Program design assumed (ideal first)

- Weekly **45-minute instrument class** in groups of **6** (one lead teacher per group), plus a weekly **60-minute supervised practice session** with up to **12 students per mentor** (a second adult is always present; the coordinator counts). That is **1.75 student-hours a week**, **63 hours** over a **36-week year** (3 terms of 12 weeks).
- Students rotate in "waves" so instruments are shared: 0.5 instrument seats per enrolled student (SEAT_RATIO).
- Instrument mix: 40% keys, 40% guitar, 20% drums and percussion.
- Every family decision about tuition below is a proposal; tuition is billed in **9 monthly payments**.

### 4.4 Formulas (paste-ready)

```
Classes        = CEILING(N/CS, 1)
Groups         = CEILING(N/PS, 1)
Lead           = Classes * LEAD_H * W * LEAD_RATE
Mentor         = Groups * PRAC_H * W * MENTOR_RATE
Coordinator    = COORD_H * (W + SETUP) * COORD_RATE
Burden         = BURDEN * (Lead + Mentor + Coordinator)
Staff          = LEADS + MENTORS + COORDS
Training       = (LEADS*LEAD_RATE + MENTORS*MENTOR_RATE + COORDS*COORD_RATE) * TRAIN_H + Staff * CERT
AVG_SEAT       = MIX_KEYS*SEAT_KEYS + MIX_GTR*SEAT_GTR + MIX_DRUM*SEAT_DRUM
FleetCost      = (CEILING(SEAT_RATIO*N,1) * AVG_SEAT + CEILING(N/48,1) * FIXED_SET) * (1 + TAX)
FleetDep       = FleetCost / LIFE
LoanerPool     = CEILING(LOAN_SHARE*N,1) * LOAN_COST * (1 + TAX)
LoanerDep      = LoanerPool / LOAN_LIFE
Materials      = MAT * N
Insurance      = INS
Background     = (Staff + VOL) * BG / BG_YEARS
Platform       = PLATFORM_MONTH * 12 / SITES
DirectCost     = Lead + Mentor + Coordinator + Burden + Training + FleetDep + LoanerDep
                 + Materials + Insurance + Background + Platform
GamoAdmin      = ADMIN_PCT * DirectCost                 (separate line; owner approval needed)
InKindRent     = RENT_RATE * ROOM_HRS * W               (cash only if RENT_RATE > 0)
TotalCost      = DirectCost + GamoAdmin + InKindRent
CostPerStudent = TotalCost / N
CostPerStudentHour = TotalCost / N / (W * 1.75)
Revenue(T)     = N * T * 9 * MIX_PAY * (1 - PROC)      (T = monthly tuition, 9 payments)
Gap(T)         = TotalCost - Revenue(T)
Coverage(T)    = Revenue(T) / TotalCost
BreakEvenT     = TotalCost / (N * 9 * MIX_PAY * (1 - PROC))
BreakEvenT_allfull = TotalCost / (N * 9 * (1 - PROC))
```

## 5. Funding and support ideas for a mission-driven PBC

**Review flag.** Everything in this section needs **counsel and accountant review** before any outreach, wording or payment flow. AGENTS.md also requires owner approval before any sponsorship, membership or similar payment processing or language. Gamo is a for-profit Public Benefit Corporation: supporters' payments are not described as tax-deductible, and nothing here should imply tax-exempt status.

1. **Mission-aligned investment.** Foundations can reach for-profit ventures through **program-related investments** (below-market loans or equity), **mission-related investments** (market-rate, aligned with the mission), and, more rarely, **grants made under "expenditure responsibility"** [S54]. A survey of 1,136 foundations that gave $984 million in 2024 found $51 million went to organizations that are not public charities plus $12 million in PRIs, which the Stanford guide reads as **under 5% of foundation funds** reaching for-profits [S54]. Grants to for-profits are typically project-based and time-bound, with a written agreement, a detailed budget, annual reports, separate tracking of funds, return of unused money, and possible intellectual-property terms [S54]. Funders known in the search summaries to use PRIs or include mission-driven for-profits: Richard King Mellon Foundation (Pittsburgh region), Sorenson Impact Foundation and Draper Richards Kaplan [S58\*]. Those eligibility statements come from search summaries and must be checked on each funder's own site.
2. **Funding that is probably closed to Gamo.** Many grant programs state they are limited to tax-exempt organizations (a Kauffman summary says this capital is typically reserved for such entities [S58\*]). Do not plan the baseline budget on them. A common workaround, **fiscal sponsorship by a tax-exempt partner**, changes who owns the program and how funds are held, and needs counsel; do not assume it is available to a PBC.
3. **Church partnership support.** Free hosting (worth **$281-$375 per student per year** at $25 a room-hour, section 4.5), custodial help, volunteer helpers (People's Music School requires 4 family hours a semester [S12]), seat support from a congregation's own budget, and a gifted-instrument drive among members. Church support must never affect musician vetting, rankings or trust signals (docs/GAMO_NONPROFIT_MODEL.md section 4).
4. **Company sponsorship.** Precedent exists in the field: a music retailer is described as the official instrument distributor of Little Kids Rock [S9\*]. For Gamo, a sponsor line would be clearly labeled, never affect vetting or rankings, and could cover instrument purchases or seats. The model's gap per seat ($277-$460 at $75 tuition) is the unit a sponsor would understand.
5. **Gifted instruments from supporters.** Brentwood Baptist accepts gifted instruments and refurbishes them for students [S22], and Gift of Music runs a $0 tier of refurbished instruments with a $25 yearly fee [S51]. Gamo can describe these as "gifted instruments from supporters" with a simple inspection, cleaning, restring and tag process; budget about $50-$100 of refurbishing per instrument (unverified [K]). Do not describe such gifts as tax-deductible.
6. **Tuition and sliding scale as the base.** At the proposed $75 standard tuition with the 60/25/15 mix, families cover about **51-63%** of direct cost; the rest is support.
7. **Instrument financing.** Retailer rent-to-own plans apply 100% of net payments to purchase at one regional retailer [S53]; Gamo could refer families to those or run its own small loaner pool (section 4).

## 6. Trade-offs when constrained (after the ideal)

The ideal above is the recommended setup. If money or volunteers force changes, these are the levers, in the order that least hurts students (judgment [K]; numbers from my model with class size 8, mentor group 15, 61-key keyboards with $100 stand, bench and pedal, loaner share 25%, a 30% shorter coordinator role):

| Site | Ideal direct cost per student | Constrained direct cost per student | Saving |
|---|---|---|---|
| Small 24 | $932 | $728 | $204 |
| Medium 48 | $831 | $650 | $181 |
| Large 96 | $750 | $570 | $180 |

Levers, with what each costs in quality:
1. **Instruments:** use 61-key touch-sensitive keyboards (PSR-E383 $169.99 [S32]) instead of 88-key weighted pianos, and buy used (Reverb used PSR-E383 $119 [S32]); the loss is touch quality and pedal technique for pianists.
2. **Class size:** 8 instead of 6 reduces lead hours by a quarter; the loss is individual attention in the first terms.
3. **Coordinator:** a trained host-church liaison volunteer for part of the load (about $167-$250 per student in the ideal) while keeping a paid coordinator for safeguarding and money.
4. **Loaners:** a smaller pool plus referral to retailer rentals at $18-$35 a month [S50][S51].
5. **Mentors:** a paid lead mentor with trained volunteers for the second adult; never reduce lead teacher pay.
6. **Term:** 30 instead of 36 weeks. Not recommended; it weakens continuity.
7. **Site:** start only with a free-hosting partner. Charging $25 an hour roughly doubles the cost at a small site.

Not on the list: deducting from musician pay, charging for items that were promised free, or letting sponsors choose teachers.

## 7. Implications for Gamo Academy

Concrete numbers the owner can use (all proposals):

- **Format to model:** one 45-minute class (6 students, 1 lead) plus one 60-minute supervised practice (up to 12 students per mentor, second adult present) per week, 36 weeks (3 terms of 12), 63 hours a year per student.
- **Direct cost per student:** **$932 (24 students), $831 (48), $750 (96)** with free hosting, owning a site fleet and a 40% take-home loaner pool, before the Gamo administrative allocation. At 10% allocation: $1,025 / $914 / $825. At cash rent of $25 per room-hour, add $281-$375.
- **Staff roles and rates:** lead $45 per paid hour (1.0 hour per class); mentors $22 (1.25 hours per group); coordinator $25 (6, 10 or 16 hours a week plus 4 setup weeks); musician pay and Gamo administration stay separate line items.
- **Tuition ladder to test:** $0 (free seat funded by support), $37.50 (half), **$75 standard** a month for nine months. At that ladder with a 60/25/15 mix, tuition covers 51% / 57% / 63% of cost; the remaining **$277-$460 per student** is the support target.
- **Break-even tuition:** about **$119-$148 a month** with the 60/25/15 mix, or **$86-$107 a month** if every family paid full price (no admin allocation).
- **Capital to raise per site:** about **$10.4k, $19.5k and $38.6k** (fleet plus loaners, including 8% tax and shipping), about $400-$434 per enrolled student; used and gifted instruments reduce this.
- **Instrument policy:** budget a take-home loaner pool at about **$100-$140 a year of depreciation per loaner**, versus **$216-$420 a year** to rent from a retailer.
- **Benchmarks to cite internally:** El Sistema USA $2,401.68 average per student in 2023-24 [S1], 71% free, median fee $263; Harmony Project $1,500 (2015) [S4]; community-school private lessons $41-$101 per lesson in 2026-27 [S14][S15]; school district music about $143-$195 per student per year [S55].
- **Operating rules to adopt now:** record attendance and retention from the first session; ask host sites for free use plus a custodial or utility contribution; require background checks for all adults; require a certificate of insurance and name the host as additional insured (typical host request [K]); publish a simple aid policy; do not accept sponsorship that affects teacher selection.
