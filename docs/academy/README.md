# Gamo Academy — operating research

Research for how Gamo Academy runs in the real world: hosted sites in churches, schools and partner venues in several cities; a family member brings the student; a 45-minute class followed by a supervised practice hour; students graduate to home practice so on-site space and instruments serve new students. Written 2026-11-16 from web research, ideal-first (limits and trade-offs are kept in a short section of each file).

**Start here:** [`OPERATING_MODEL.md`](OPERATING_MODEL.md) combines the seven research files into one recommended model, lists where they disagree, and lists the decisions the owner must make. All numbers in it are proposals until approved (fees, tiers and contributions never take effect without owner approval; musician and instructor pay and any Gamo administrative allocation are always separate lines).

## How to read the evidence labels

`[S]` read in a source this session; `[S*]`/`[S†]`/`[S~]` seen only as a search summary (page blocked); `[K]` prior knowledge or judgment; `[M]` modeled by the author; `(unverified)` not confirmed. The research agents ran out of search budget before a second verification pass, so treat anything not marked `[S]` as a starting point to confirm, not as a fact to publish.
